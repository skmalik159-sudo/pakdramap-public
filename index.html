What this application is

PakDramaP YouTube Automation is a small, private automation tool. It is used by one operator to schedule and publish that operator's own video content to a single YouTube channel that they own and control.

Who uses it

Only the operator of the application. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, no user accounts and no customers. There is no version of this application that other people log into.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules videos the operator has produced, to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads back the status of those uploads so the operator can confirm a scheduled publish succeeded.
  • Reads publicly available information about television broadcast schedules to decide when to publish.

Google account access

The application asks the operator's own Google account for two YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload and schedule the operator's own videos.
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm which channel is authorised and to read back the status of those uploads.

It requests no other Google data, and it accesses no Google account other than the operator's own.
